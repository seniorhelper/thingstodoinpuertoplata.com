About Things To Do in Puerto Plata

Updated November 10, 2026

Puerto Plata and the Amber Coast offer a different Dominican Republic from the big eastern resorts: the cable car up Mount Isabel de Torres, the Damajagua waterfalls, the Victorian center, and Sosúa and Cabarete close by. This guide helps you plan what to do and when.

Who publishes it

Published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We're not a tour company.

How we work

  • We don't make up reviews, prices or ratings.
  • Opening hours and conditions change, so confirm with the operator before you go.
